The smart training partner who knows your body and respects your life.

Every screen in the Pulse direction, keeping your wine. One bold wine field for whatever matters now, big confident type, and motion that shows VIV responding to her. The two phones below are live: tap through the daily loop.

One wine field per screen

Whatever is due now sits in the only filled block, with a pulsing dot. Everything else is a line. She never decides what matters.

Propose, never impose

The old plan stays visible, struck through, and “Keep the original” is one tap. Her choice is tagged “your call”.

Skipping is valid

No check-in: “That’s fine, you’re on your usual plan.” A missed session moves; it isn’t counted against her.

Motion

Check-in rises over home (460 ms). Each answer pops into a chip and the next question slides in. Proposal: strike the old plan at 820 ms, reveal the new at 1150 ms. See the live phones at the top.
